Project 4: Restaurants (nomnom)

A table of restaurants called nomnom needs your help to find the best dinner spots in the city. Open nomnom.db and start by running SELECT * FROM nomnom;.

Dataset: open nomnom.db, table nomnom — columns: name, neighborhood, cuisine, review, price, health.
  1. What are the distinct neighborhoods?
  2. What are the distinct cuisine types?
  3. Suppose we would like some Chinese takeout. What are our options?
  4. Return all the restaurants with reviews of 4 and above.
  5. Abbi and Ilana want a fancy dinner date. Return all the restaurants that are Italian and $$$.
  6. Your coworker Trey cannot remember the exact name of a restaurant, but it contains the word meatball. Find it with a query.
  7. Let us order delivery! Find all nearby spots in Midtown, Downtown or Chinatown. (Hint: OR can be used more than once.)
  8. Find all the restaurants whose health grade is pending (empty values).
  9. Create a Top 10 Restaurants ranking based on reviews.
  10. Use a CASE statement to change the rating system: review above 4.5 is Extraordinary; above 4 is Excellent; above 3 is Good; above 2 is Fair; everything else is Poor. Do not forget to rename the new column!
