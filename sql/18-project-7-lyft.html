Project 7: Lyft

On this page
  • Queries and aggregates

You are a Data Analyst at Lyft, a ride-sharing platform. You were given three tables: trips (trip information), riders (user data) and cars (autonomous cars). Open lyft.db.

  1. Examine the three tables. What are the column names? (SELECT * FROM trips;, SELECT * FROM riders;, SELECT * FROM cars;)
  2. What is the primary key of trips? Of riders? Of cars? (It is id each time.)
  3. Try a simple cross join between riders and cars (for instance the rider names and the car models). Is the result useful?
  4. Create a Trip Log with the trips and their users. Find the columns to join trips and riders and combine them with a LEFT JOIN, with trips as the left table.
  5. Link the trips to the cars used during them. Find the columns to join trips and cars and combine them with an INNER JOIN.
  6. New rider data has arrived: three new users this month, stored in a table riders2. Stack riders on top of riders2.

Queries and aggregates

  • What is the average cost of a trip?
  • Lyft wants an email campaign for irregular users. Find all riders who have used Lyft fewer than 500 times.
  • Calculate the number of cars that are active.
  • It is safety recall time for cars that have been on the road a long while. Write a query that finds the two cars with the highest trips_completed.
