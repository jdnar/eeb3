Warm-up 4: Aggregate Functions

Practice database: Download pets-owners.db or the SQL setup script. If you open the prebuilt database, skip the setup script.

One calculation for each function of Chapter 4, still on the pets table.

Tip: Type every statement yourself and run it after each step.

Step 4.1 — COUNT

How many pets are in the table?

Expected result:

COUNT(*)
8

Step 4.2 — SUM

What is the sum of the ages of all pets?

Expected result:

SUM(age)
33

Step 4.3 — MAX and MIN

What are the greatest and the smallest ages? (Do it in one query.)

Expected result:

MAX(age) | MIN(age)
9 | 1

Step 4.4 — AVG

What is the average age of the pets?

Expected result:

AVG(age)
4.125

Step 4.5 — ROUND

What is the average weight, rounded to 1 decimal?

Expected result:

ROUND(AVG(weight), 1)
8.8

Step 4.6 — GROUP BY

How many pets are there of each species?

Expected result:

species | COUNT(*)
bird | 1
cat | 3
dog | 3
fish | 1

Step 4.7 — GROUP BY with column numbers

Show each species with its average age (rounded to 1 decimal), from the highest to the lowest average. Use column reference numbers in GROUP BY and ORDER BY.

Expected result:

species | ROUND(AVG(age), 1)
dog | 5.0
cat | 4.7
bird | 3.0
fish | 1.0

Step 4.8 — HAVING

Show only the species that have more than one pet, with their number of pets.

Expected result:

species | COUNT(*)
cat | 3
dog | 3
