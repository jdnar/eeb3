Exercise 13: Split and Join

Save it as exercise13. Write a program that:

  • creates a variable text with the value "Hello,everybody,how,are,you,doing?";
  • splits text on the , delimiter and assigns the result to result;
  • prints result;
  • joins the list back with a space delimiter into a variable result2;
  • prints result2.
Output
['Hello', 'everybody', 'how', 'are', 'you', 'doing?']
Hello everybody how are you doing?
Tip: To split on a # character: my_text = my_text.split("#"). To join on a ,: my_list = ",".join(my_list).
