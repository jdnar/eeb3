Warm-up 9: For Loop

On this page

Practice for, sequences and the range() function.

Tip: Do each step yourself and check your result.

Step 9.1 — Letters

Print each letter of the word "python" on its own line.

Expected output:

Output
p
y
t
h
o
n

Step 9.2 — range(5)

Print the values produced by range(5).

Expected output:

Output
0
1
2
3
4

Step 9.3 — Even numbers

Print the even numbers from 2 to 10 included, using the three-parameter form of range.

Expected output:

Output
2
4
6
8
10

Step 9.4 — Countdown with range

Print 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 using range with a negative step.

Expected output:

Output
5
4
3
2
1

Step 9.5 — Sum from 1 to 10

Use a for loop and a variable total to add the numbers from 1 to 10 and print the result.

Expected output:

Output
55

Step 9.6 — Mini pyramid

Print a pyramid of * with 4 rows, using a loop and string multiplication.

Expected output:

Output
*
**
***
****

Step 9.7 — Going through a list

For each name in the list ["Ana", "Luis", "Sofia"], print Hello followed by the name.

Expected output:

Output
Hello Ana
Hello Luis
Hello Sofia
