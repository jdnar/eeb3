Warm-up 3: Variables

On this page
  • Step 3.1 — Assign and display
  • Step 3.2 — Valid names?
  • Step 3.3 — Case sensitive
  • Step 3.4 — Update a variable
  • Step 3.5 — Text or variable?

Practice the assignment name = value, the naming rules and updating a variable.

Tip: Do each step yourself before looking at the answer. Solutions: Appendix B.3.

Step 3.1 — Assign and display

In the shell, create name = "Ana" and age = 15. Then type name and age on their own lines. What do you see?

Step 3.2 — Valid names?

Which of these are valid variable names? my_var, 2cool, Age, total_2, my-var, my var, _x.

Step 3.3 — Case sensitive

Write a script with x = 5 and X = 10 and print x + X. (x and X are two different variables.)

Expected output:

Output
15

Step 3.4 — Update a variable

Start with score = 10. Add 5 to the score and print it; then double the score and print it again.

Expected output:

Output
15
30

Step 3.5 — Text or variable?

After colour = "red", what do colour and "colour" display in the shell?
