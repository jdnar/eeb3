Warm-up 4: Operators

On this page
  • Step 4.1 — Arithmetic in the shell
  • Step 4.2 — Modulus by hand
  • Step 4.3 — Quotient by hand
  • Step 4.4 — Even or odd?
  • Step 4.5 — Use variables

Work with the arithmetic operators of Chapter 4, including % and //.

Tip: Do each step yourself before looking at the answer. Solutions: Appendix B.4.

Step 4.1 — Arithmetic in the shell

Predict, then check: 7 + 3 * 2, (7 + 3) * 2, 2 ** 5, 15 / 4.

Step 4.2 — Modulus by hand

Find the remainder without a computer: 29 % 6, 40 % 8, 7 % 10. Then check.

Step 4.3 — Quotient by hand

Find the truncated quotient without a computer: 29 // 6, 40 // 8, 7 // 10. Then check.

Step 4.4 — Even or odd?

Which operator can tell you whether a number is even? Evaluate 14 % 2 and 15 % 2 and explain.

Step 4.5 — Use variables

Write a script with width = 4 and height = 6 that prints the area and the perimeter of the rectangle on one line.

Expected output:

Output
24 20
