Warm-up 2: Data Types

On this page
  • Step 2.1 — Name the type
  • Step 2.2 — Check with type()
  • Step 2.3 — Number or text?

Short exercises on the three data types of Chapter 2: int, float and str.

Tip: Do each step yourself before looking at the answer. Solutions: Appendix B.2.

Step 2.1 — Name the type

Is each value an int, a float or a str? 42, 3.14, "42", 'hello', -8, 7.0, "7.5".

Step 2.2 — Check with type()

Check three of your answers in the shell with type(), for example type(42). What does type("42") show, and why?

Step 2.3 — Number or text?

Predict the result of 8 + 2 and of "8" + "2", then check in the shell.
