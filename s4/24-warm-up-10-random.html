Warm-up 10: Random

On this page
  • Step 10.1 — Roll a die
  • Step 10.2 — Flip a coin
  • Step 10.3 — A random float
  • Step 10.4 — Five rolls
  • Step 10.5 — Which values?

Because the results are random, there is no fixed expected output: run each program several times.

Tip: Do each step yourself before looking at the answer. Solutions: Appendix B.10.

Step 10.1 — Roll a die

Import random and print a random integer between 1 and 6 (a die roll).

Step 10.2 — Flip a coin

Print heads or tails at random using random.choice.

Step 10.3 — A random float

Print a random decimal number between 0 and 1.

Step 10.4 — Five rolls

Combine a for loop and random.randint to print five die rolls.

Step 10.5 — Which values?

Which values can random.randint(3, 7) return? Is 7 included?
