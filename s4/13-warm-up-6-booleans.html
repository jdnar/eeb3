Warm-up 6: Booleans

On this page
  • Step 6.1 — Compare in the shell
  • Step 6.2 — Truth by hand
  • Step 6.3 — = or ==?
  • Step 6.4 — Combine conditions
  • Step 6.5 — Predict

Practice comparison operators and and, or, not.

Tip: Do each step yourself and check your result.

Step 6.1 — Compare in the shell

In the shell, set x = 10, then predict and check: x > 5, x == 10, x != 10, x <= 9.

Step 6.2 — Truth by hand

Predict: True and False, True or False, not True, not (3 > 5).

Step 6.3 — = or ==?

Which of these lines assigns a value and which compares two values? a = 3, a == 3, a != 3, a = a + 1.

Step 6.4 — Combine conditions

With age = 15, write one expression that is True for teenagers (from 12 to 17 years old included). Test it with age = 20 too.

Step 6.5 — Predict

With a = 4 and b = 9, predict: a > b or b > a, not a == 4, a < b and b < 5.
